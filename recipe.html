FROM THE SAVOURÉ KITCHEN

Hyderabadi chicken
biryani, made slowly.

Fragrant basmati, tender chicken and whole spices—one timeless dish, cooked with patience.

◷ 1 hr 15 min♨ Medium◉ Serves 4
4K RECIPE FILM

Watch the saffron rice, marinated chicken and fragrant dum come together.

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, bone-in
  • 2 cups aged basmati rice
  • Fried onions & fresh mint
  • Yogurt, ginger and garlic
  • Saffron milk & ghee
  • Cardamom, clove, cinnamon

THE METHOD

Build flavour,
one layer at a time.

01

Marinate the chicken

Coat the chicken in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli, turmeric and biryani masala. Rest for at least 30 minutes.

02

Par-cook the rice

Boil the basmati with whole spices until it is 70% cooked. Drain gently so every grain stays long and separate.

03

Layer and dum

Layer chicken and rice with fried onions, mint, ghee and saffron milk. Seal the pot and cook on low heat for 25 minutes.

04

Rest, fluff and serve

Let it rest for ten minutes, then lift from the bottom so the saffron, chicken and rice meet on every plate.
